Questions, answered

What to know before you begin.

What is Musing?

A private notes app for iPhone, Mac, and Apple Watch. Capture text, voice, photos, links, and places, then retrieve them by asking or let them return on place visits and calendar anniversaries.

How do Place Recall and Time Recall work?

Place Recall uses geofenced regions on your device to notice when you return to a place you've previously written about. Time Recall checks calendar anniversaries (1, 2, 3+ years ago) and seasonal milestones. Everything is computed 100% on-device with quiet morning delivery (at most twice a week), and sensitive locations like hospitals are automatically suppressed.

Does Musing work on Apple Watch?

Yes. Musing includes a native Apple Watch app. You can dictate thoughts or capture ideas directly from your wrist with Watch complications, and notes sync instantly to your iPhone and Mac via your private iCloud.

Do I need folders or tags?

No. Musing keeps useful context automatically, so you can remember a note by its words, place, weather, time, or type without maintaining a filing system.

Is Musing really free?

Yes. There are no ads, accounts, subscriptions, credits, or paid unlock packs.

Does anything leave my device?

By default, no. Note text, photos, and queries stay on-device; Ask and understanding use Apple Intelligence where available. Your private iCloud can sync notes between your devices. Weather uses Apple WeatherKit, and shared links fetch a preview from the website you save. The only optional exception is Pro Mode web search, below.

What is Pro Mode?

Bring your own AI service. Turn on Pro Mode, paste your own OpenAI or Anthropic API key, and ask Musing to look something up on the web — from a note, from Ask, or while writing. You choose the provider and control its costs. Only that requested lookup is sent, with cited sources returned. Off by default; nothing is sold inside Musing.

Does it work on Mac and Apple Watch?

Yes. Musing is native on iPhone, Mac, and Apple Watch, with your notes synced through your private iCloud when enabled. On Mac, Control-Option-Command-N writes or Asks from any app — no Accessibility permission. The menu bar still captures; Reading Room is for asking and browsing.

Do I need Apple Intelligence?

Apple Intelligence powers Ask, understanding, and semantic recall. Notes, Place Recall, Time Recall, and standard text search remain available without it.
