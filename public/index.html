How it works

A notebook, not a database.

No folders, no tags to manage, no sorting. Notes rest in a quiet ledger, and come back the way you remember them — “coffee shop notes,” “rainy day ideas,” “notes from Traverse City” — because every note kept its moment.

The ledger

Every note is a line in your notebook — the words, then the place and hour in the margin. A small dot carries the kind; nothing is ever labeled or filed.

Just ask

Questions get answers, grounded in your own notes and set on paper. Ask by the moment, too — place, weather, time of day, kind of place — alone or combined: “receipts from Oak Park last month.” Instant, even in airplane mode.

Every capture quietly absorbs its moment — neighborhood, weather, time of day — so “that note from the rainy café” actually finds it.

Two taps to capture

Type, speak, snap, or just check in. Photos are read on your iPhone, so a receipt or a book cover is findable later. “Remind me Tuesday at 9” becomes a real Apple Reminder, linked to the note.

Free · Private · On your iPhone

Musing is free — no ads, no account, no subscription, no credits. Your notes never leave your device, except to sync through your own private iCloud. Search, answers, and every bit of understanding run on your iPhone with Apple Intelligence — no server ever reads a note, nothing trains a model, and every context signal has an off switch.
